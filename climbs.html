Popular Climbs

A selection of classic mountain routes for different skill levels.

Mount Fuji

Japan · Intermediate · 3,776 m

Japan’s iconic peak. Best climbed in July–September. Well-marked trails and mountain huts along the way.

Ben Nevis

Scotland · Beginner–Intermediate · 1,345 m

The highest mountain in the UK. The tourist path is popular; winter conditions demand experience.

Mount Rainier

USA · Advanced · 4,392 m

Glacier travel and crevasse risk. Requires technical skills, permits, and proper gear.

Kilimanjaro

Tanzania · Intermediate · 5,895 m

Africa’s highest mountain. Multiple routes; altitude is the main challenge rather than technical climbing.
