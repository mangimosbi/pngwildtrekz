Climbing Tips

Simple advice to help you stay safe and enjoy the mountains.

1. Plan Ahead

Check weather, trail conditions, and daylight hours. Tell someone your route and expected return time.

2. Pack Essentials

Water, food, map/GPS, first-aid kit, extra layers, headlamp, and a fully charged phone (or power bank).

3. Dress in Layers

Base layer, insulating mid-layer, and waterproof shell. Avoid cotton — it stays wet and cold.

4. Know Your Limits

Start with easier routes. Turn back if conditions worsen or you feel unwell. The mountain will still be there.

5. Respect the Environment

Stay on trails, pack out all trash, and leave no trace so others can enjoy the same wild places.

6. Learn Basic Skills

Practice navigation, rope work (if needed), and how to use an ice axe or crampons before tackling steeper terrain.
